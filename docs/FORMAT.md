# AEL competition format

## Constitutional rule

> The league phase decides your competition. The knockout phase decides your champion.

## League phase

Domestic football qualifies 108 clubs. Initial strength tiers control fixture construction:

| Tier | Matches |
|---|---:|
| A | 8 |
| B | 7 |
| C | 6 |

The unified standings then determine:

| AEL finish | Destination |
|---|---|
| 1–36 | UCL |
| 37–72 | UEL |
| 73–108 | UECL |

First place receives the All-Europe Shield.

## January ladder

The ladder is identical within UCL, UEL and UECL:

| Band finish | Route |
|---|---|
| 1–8 | Direct to Round of 16 |
| 9–16 | Final Playoff; one win required |
| 17–28 | Qualification Round; two wins required |
| 29–36 | Wild Card Round; three wins required |

All January ties are single matches hosted by the higher AEL finisher. The principal knockout competition begins in February with a two-legged Round of 16.

## No parachutes

Once the AEL standings assign a club to UCL, UEL or UECL, it remains in that competition. The January backdoor is a harder route within an earned destination, not relegation into a different competition.
